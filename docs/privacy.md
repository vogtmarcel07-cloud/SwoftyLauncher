# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what Swofty Launcher and its websites collect and how it is used. The launcher is provided by Swofty.

## 1. Usage data from the launcher

Release builds of the launcher send anonymised usage reports while the launcher is open and while the game is running through it. A report contains:

- a random install identifier created on your computer, which is not linked to your name or game account;
- the launcher version, your operating system and whether the game is the Steam or EA app version;
- when a game session starts and ends, and the exit code when it ends.

We use this to count active installs, see which versions are in use and measure crash rates.

## 2. Information you choose to send

- **Logs.** If you press Send Logs, the launcher sends its log files, the name you enter and your description of the problem, so that we can diagnose it.
- **Accounts and leaderboards.** If you sign in to the launcher, we store your account name and the laps and replays you submit, and show them on public leaderboards.
- **Addon publishing.** If you publish an addon, we store your publisher account details and the addon you upload.

## 3. Advertising

The launcher shows advertising through a web page that it loads in an embedded browser panel. Advertising is provided by RevIQ and its advertising partners, which include Google. When an advert loads, these partners may:

- receive your IP address, browser type and approximate location;
- set or read cookies and similar identifiers;
- use that information to choose, limit and measure adverts, and to detect fraud.

Where the law requires consent for personalised advertising, you are asked before it is used, and you can decline. Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can also manage interest-based advertising at [youradchoices.com](https://youradchoices.com/) and [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell the usage data described in section 1.
- We do not collect your game account password or payment details.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data

Usage reports are kept in aggregate form for statistics. You can ask for data linked to your launcher account, logs you sent or addons you published to be deleted.

## 6. Children

The launcher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 8. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be raised on the [project's GitHub page](https://github.com/Swofty-Developments/SwoftyLauncher).
