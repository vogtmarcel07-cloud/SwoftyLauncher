# 1.9.43

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.43 fixes careers with more than 24 races losing rounds when they load.

## Career calendars

- A career whose calendar has more than 24 races keeps all of them when it loads. Since 1.9.24, the check that repairs a calendar list blown up by repeated entries took 24 as every season's length, so a longer calendar lost its extra rounds when the career loaded. The season kept its full number of races, so rounds past the 24th never came and the season couldn't finish. The check now goes by the races the career's calendar actually has.
- A list blown up by repeated entries is still collapsed back to its season, as before.
- The calendar line in the log now names the career's number of race weekends.

A career that already lost rounds can't get them back from the save. If it is still before its 24th race, **Repair the round count** on the Career Mode Editor's calendar page removes the rounds that will never come, so the season finishes after round 24.
